## Background

A small Astro site that collects and redistributes NASA's public imagery from
the Artemis II mission as free desktop and phone wallpapers. It crops and
re-exports the raw mission photos at common screen resolutions, serves them
statically from GitHub Pages, and makes the set easy to browse and download.
