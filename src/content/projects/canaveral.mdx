## Background

canaveral is a Rust workspace that drives release workflows across the
different kinds of packages I ship: npm modules, crates, Cloudflare Workers,
iOS and macOS apps. It bumps versions, writes changelogs from conventional
commits, tags, publishes, and kicks off the right downstream pipelines per
target. The goal is one command from "main is green" to "users have the new
build" — no release-day rituals to remember.
