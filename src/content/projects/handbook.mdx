## Background

The SevenTwo Studio handbook is the public-ish artifact of how I want my
company to build software: opinionated tech choices (Bun, Hono, Astro, Drizzle,
Better Auth, Biome), process defaults (conventional commits, result types
instead of throws, testing against real implementations), and the reasoning
behind each. It's an Astro + MDX site so entries are easy to write, easy to
link, and version-controlled alongside the rest of the org.
