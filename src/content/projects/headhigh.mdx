## Background

HeadHigh is a published iOS app under SevenTwo Studio that turns AirPods'
built-in motion sensors into a real-time posture coach. It reads head
orientation from the headset, learns a neutral baseline, and nudges you when
your neck drifts forward. The whole thing runs on-device in SwiftUI with no
backend — the data never leaves the phone.
