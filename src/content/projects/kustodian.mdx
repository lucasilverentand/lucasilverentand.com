## Background

kustodian is a TypeScript CLI (Bun) that takes declarative YAML templates and
compiles them into ready-to-apply Kubernetes manifests that Flux CD can sync.
It sits in the gap between raw Kustomize and full-blown Helm: enough structure
to share primitives across clusters (homelab, prod, staging), but with
plugins so I can drop in new shapes without forking the tool. It's what drives
the Kubernetes side of my homelab in the silverswarm org.
