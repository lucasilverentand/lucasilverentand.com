## Background

pulse is the working codename for Seven, an AI-native version control system
I'm building under SevenTwo Studio. The premise: today's VCS was designed for
humans typing commits, but most of the code flowing through my repos is now
produced by agents. Seven rethinks the primitives for that world — richer
intent metadata on changes, first-class multi-agent coordination, and a
history model that stays legible when dozens of parallel branches land a day.
Written in Rust for performance and long-lived correctness.
